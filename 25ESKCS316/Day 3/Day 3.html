About SKIT

Swami Keshvanand Institute of Technology, Management & Gramothan

Our History

Established with a vision to provide quality technical education, SKIT has become one of Rajasthan's leading engineering institutions. The institute has consistently produced skilled professionals with excellent academic and placement records.

Vision & Mission

Vision: To promote quality education, innovation, and research globally.

Mission: To provide an excellent academic environment and prepare students for future technological challenges.

Campus Facilities

Modern Classrooms

Air-conditioned smart classrooms equipped with digital teaching facilities and multimedia projectors.

Central Library

Thousands of books, journals, research papers and digital learning resources.

High-Tech Laboratories

Advanced laboratories equipped with modern instruments for practical learning and research.
